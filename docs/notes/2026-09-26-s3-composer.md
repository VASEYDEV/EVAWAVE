## Decisions made while building

- **Instrument bank scope.** The generator reads only the seed's enumerable sections: GM
  programs and percussion, the drum-machine table and the world-set lists. The seed's
  §4–§6 are prose lists whose names lack their family noun ("12-string", "electric
  humbucker"), and they mix in techniques. Generating records from them would make up
  data. They wait for curation batches, as the seed's own §10 orders.
- **Merges, not duplicates.** A seed item that names an instrument already in the bank is
  merged and listed in `manifest.json`: 41 merges today. Matching is by id, name or common
  name, singular or plural, plus two explicit rules:
  - `steelpan` is GM "Steel Drums";
  - "tar (frame drum)" stays distinct from the tar lute.

  Drum-machine kits match on their phrase only, so the 808 kit never merges into the 808
  sub kick.
- **Honest families.** World-set instruments get `family: 'percussion'` only when the
  seed lists them under percussion. The rest are `other` until curation. Every generated
  record starts `unverified` on every engine, as the seed says.
- **Typing is one undo step per field.** Coalescing keys on the IR path and ends at the
  next other edit, undo or redo.
- **The target switch is an IR edit.** `D10.activeTarget` is in the spec, so switching
  target is undoable. It never touches anything else (e2e check).
- **Playwright's browser.** The locked Playwright is 1.63. This environment ships Chromium
  build 1194, so `PLAYWRIGHT_CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE` points the config at it. CI installs the
  matching browser.
